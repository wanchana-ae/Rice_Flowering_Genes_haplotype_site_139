4 · How the SNPs were selected

  1. Window. All SNPs and indels in each gene plus 2 kb upstream and 2 kb downstream (IRGSP-1.0 coordinates, RAP-DB 2026-02-05; strand-aware), from the WGS joint VCF (625 accessions).
  2. Quality filter (within the 139 accessions): a site is kept if genotypes are missing in ≤5% of accessions, heterozygous in ≤5%, and the minor-allele frequency is ≥5%.
  3. Tag SNPs. Within each gene, sites are ordered by quality (fewest missing/heterozygous calls, then higher MAF). A site is dropped if its composite (Rogers–Huff) r² with an already kept site is ≥0.8, so each kept site represents its LD partners. Phenotype was not used in this step.
  4. Haplotype per gene. The string of homozygous genotypes at the tag sites. Accessions that are heterozygous or missing at any tag site have no haplotype for that gene and are omitted from combinations including it (genotypes are unphased).
  5. Merging similar haplotypes. A haplotype carried by fewer than 5 accessions is merged into the nearest haplotype with n≥5 if the two differ at ≤2 tag sites; otherwise it stays separate. This can hide a functional difference carried by a single site.
  6. Combined haplotype = the per-gene haplotype groups of the core block and of the added gene together; groups with n≥5 are tested. The settings (r² 0.8, ≤2 sites, missing ≤5%) were fixed before looking at the results.
